Leadership Profile

A Vision Built on
Trust & Purpose

Mr Sirajudeen Bin Syed Mohamed founded Royal Kings Group in Singapore in 2013 with a clear and enduring vision — to build a business platform that goes beyond transactions, creating a genuine ecosystem of value for every client, partner and community the Group touches.

Under his leadership, the Group has grown from a single wholesale trading entity into a fully integrated five-pillar business platform recognised across Singapore and the Asia-Pacific region — spanning technology, consultancy, hospitality, global trade and financial technology.

Mr Sirajudeen is also the CEO of Nambikai Singapore and a deeply committed advocate for community welfare, interfaith harmony and social responsibility. His leadership philosophy — rooted in transparency, performance and purpose — has been recognised with over 15 national and international business awards.

"We do not simply offer services — we invest in the growth of every business we work with. That is the Royal Kings commitment."

— Mr. Sirajudeen Bin Syed Mohamed, Founder & Chairman
Community Leadership

Giving Back to Singapore & Beyond

Royal Kings Group believes sustainable business success is inseparable from positive community impact. Under Mr. Sirajudeen's leadership, the Group actively contributes to Singapore's social fabric through three pillars of community engagement:

🎓

Education & Skills

Supporting youth entrepreneurship programmes, skills development workshops and mentorship initiatives equipping the next generation of Asian business leaders.

🌱

Community Development

Active participation in community outreach, charitable initiatives and partnerships — including sponsorship of interfaith Iftar gatherings and national day celebrations.

♻️

Sustainable Business

Embedding responsible business practices from ethical supply chain management to contributing to Singapore's Green Plan 2030 and green-office initiatives.
